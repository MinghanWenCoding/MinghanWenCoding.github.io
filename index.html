About Us

At BURGER PRINCE, we believe a great burger doesn’t need fancy gimmicks—just 100% locally sourced, never-frozen beef smashed to perfection on a screaming-hot flat-top grill. Founded in 2024 by two lifelong friends with a shared obsession for crispy edges and melted cheddar, our kitchen builds every order from scratch on freshly baked brioche buns.
